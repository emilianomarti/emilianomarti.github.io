Check out our latest paper in Genetics! We generated a detailed repetitive sequence and epigenetic landscape of the B chromosome in Drosophila melanogaster. We found novel satellite DNAs and confirmed that these supernumerary chromosomes are heavily heterochromatic and transcriptionally silent.
